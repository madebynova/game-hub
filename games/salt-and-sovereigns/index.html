Salt & Sovereigns

A top-down pirate trading venture for a free period.

Steer with WASD or the Arrows — your ship carries momentum, so plan your turns. Sail into any of the 8 port islands and ease off as you touch the harbour to open the trading window.

Buy low, sell high. Prices are frozen while you sail; they only move when you Rest & Advance Time in a port. Reefs gash your hull — mind the pale rocks.

Goal: build a Fortune — a net worth of 1,000,000 Sovereigns. Passing 10,000 just makes you an established trader; the real voyage is everything after.

Press M for the charts, Esc to pause, H to hide the HUD. Full details are in the 📖 Manual below.
