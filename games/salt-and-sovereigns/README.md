## 🌊 Welcome Aboard

**Salt & Sovereigns** is a small, single-file HTML5 pirate trading game where you take the helm, sail between ports, buy low, sell high, manage your ship, and decide how much trouble you're willing to get yourself into.

The current build combines:

* ⚓ Open-water sailing
* 🏝️ Multiple ports to discover
* 💰 Buy-low / sell-high trading
* 📦 Cargo management
* 🗺️ Full interactive charts
* 🏴‍☠️ Pirate havens
* 👑 Crown ports
* ⚠️ Contraband and smuggling
* ⭐ Wanted levels
* 🚢 Navy patrols
* 🛠️ Ship repairs and upgrades
* 🏆 Captain leaderboard
* 💾 Browser saving
* 📦 Export / Import friend codes
* ⏸️ Pause + Quick Save
* 📜 Change log
* 🌊 Momentum-based ship movement

The game is intentionally still evolving.

**If something feels weird, unbalanced, confusing, too easy, too hard, or just plain fun — I want to know.**

# 🎮 How To Play

### 🧭 Movement

Use:

**W A S D**

or

**Arrow Keys**

Your ship has momentum, so movement isn't instant. Accelerate, brake, turn, and learn how the ship handles.

### 🗺️ Map

Press:

**M**

to open the full charts.

The map shows ports, commodities, prices, and useful information for planning your next voyage.

### ⏸️ Pause

Press:

**ESC**

to pause the game.

From the pause menu you can:

* Resume
* **Quick Save**
* Return to the main menu

### 💾 Need to close the game quickly?

**Press ESC → Quick Save.**

This is especially useful if you need to close the game quickly at school.

# 💰 Trade & Build Your Fortune

Sail between ports and look for profitable trades.

Some goods will be cheaper at certain ports while being more valuable elsewhere.

Your goal is to build your fortune and eventually reach:

### 🏆 10,000 Sovereigns

But that's only the beginning.

The current game includes a larger risk/reward system through smuggling.

---

# 🏴‍☠️ Smuggling

Some cargo isn't exactly... legal.

Contraband can provide **much larger profits**, but carrying it comes with consequences.

If the Navy catches you, you may have to:

* 💰 Bribe officials
* 🗝️ Hide cargo
* 🏴‍☠️ Flee

Your actions can increase your **Wanted Level**, causing Navy patrols to come after you.

The higher your heat gets, the more dangerous the waters become.

---

# 🚢 Navy & Wanted System

Getting caught doesn't necessarily mean the end.

But if you're on the Navy's list...

One of the main features of this testing build is the ability to share your progress with friends.
